Getting to the Domaine du Geisberg

Varsberg lies between Saint-Avold and Creutzwald, 40 minutes from Metz and 30 minutes from Saarbrücken.

By car: A4 motorway (Paris–Strasbourg), exit Saint-Avold / Carling, then follow Creutzwald and Varsberg.

By train: Saint-Avold station 6 km away (Metz–Forbach regional line).
