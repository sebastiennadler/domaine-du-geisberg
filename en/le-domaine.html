Holiday gîtes · Varsberg · Moselle

The estate

Set in the heart of a vast two-hectare estate of green meadows at the edge of the forest, our two gîtes occupy the ground floor of a spacious house in an exceptional spot known as “Vogelsgesang” (birdsong).

Typical of the Lorraine countryside, the surroundings are lush and offer a beautiful panorama in a setting of calm and serenity.

There are many hiking trails, reachable without crossing a road, on foot, by bike or on horseback. Horses are available for experienced riders.
