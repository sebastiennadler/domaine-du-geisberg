The estate

A place that lives up to its name

Our two gîtes are two apartments on the garden level of our house, in the heart of a vast two-hectare estate of green meadows at the edge of the forest, in an exceptional spot known as “Vogelsgesang”, birdsong.

Typical of the Lorraine countryside, the surroundings are lush and offer a beautiful panorama in a setting of calm and serenity.
