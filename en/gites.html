Holiday cottages · Varsberg · Moselle

The cottages

Two similar semi-detached cottages at garden level, each with a separate entrance, private terrace and parking.

Each cottage has a living room with fitted kitchen (oven, ceramic hob, microwave, dishwasher, washing machine, freezer), two bedrooms and a shower room with WC, adapted for guests with reduced mobility. Sleeping: four 90 cm beds that can be pushed together, plus a sofa in the living room. Flat-screen TV, wifi, geothermal underfloor heating.
