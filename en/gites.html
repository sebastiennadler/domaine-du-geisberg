Holiday gîtes · Varsberg · Moselle

The gîtes

Two similar holiday apartments at garden level of the owners' house, each with a separate entrance, private terrace and parking.

Each gîte has a living room with fitted kitchen (oven, ceramic hob, microwave, dishwasher, washing machine, freezer), two bedrooms and a shower room with WC, adapted for guests with reduced mobility. Sleeping: four 90 cm beds that can be pushed together in the bedrooms, plus a fifth bed in the living room: 5 guests per gîte. Flat-screen TV, wifi, geothermal underfloor heating.

The two gîtes are twins: same rooms, same equipment, same view over the meadow. Simply choose whichever is free on your dates — or both for a larger group.
